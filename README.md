# Chalkboard
A custom playbook, formation and play editor for CFB27. Build offense and defense books, draw up your own formations and plays, set the gameplan (coach suggestion weights and AI playcalling weights), and the tool writes the mod + playbook save for you. Books can also be set to replace a CPU team's playbook. Meaning you can make Northwestern run the triple option.

**Download the latest version:** https://github.com/JWDixon12/ChalkboardRelease/releases/latest

## HOW TO INSTALL
1. Download `Chalkboard-1.0.0-setup.exe` from the link above and run it.
2. Windows may show a blue "Windows protected your PC" box since the app isn't signed. Click **More info** then **Run anyway**.
3. Pick where to install it and finish. It drops a Chalkboard shortcut on your desktop.
4. You need the **MMC Mod Manager** (v1.1.0.6 or newer) to load the mods Chalkboard builds.
5. First time you open **Build & Export**, point it at your Mod Manager's CollegeFB27 mods folder and your CFB27 Saves folder (Documents\EA SPORTS College Football 27\Saves). It looks for both on its own and shows what it found.

**Using a book in game:** hit Build in Build & Export, open the Mod Manager, enable the Chalkboard mod it made, apply and launch. Only have one Chalkboard mod on at a time. Your book shows up with your custom playbooks in game.

**Updates:** Chalkboard checks for updates on its own every time you open it. When a new version is out it pops up, you click to download and it installs itself. No need to come back here and reinstall. Your books stay where they are (Settings has a button to open the books folder if you want to back them up).

**After a game patch:** wait for the Chalkboard update that matches the patch, then run Patch Check on your books before building again.

Chalkboard is a free fan-made tool, not affiliated with EA SPORTS.
